it was the month of silver
one day before growing out yellow leaves,
a spiral staircase erupted from the clouds
to the top of a bottomless pit with
birds framed with black,
branches framed with vines
twisting towards its lukewarm roots.
climbing it was easier done than said,
like saying i love you eight times.
an endless orbit around blue gave chase,
loss of milk teeth cleared the sky
and the sun shined bright.
on the last day of our tenth guitar solo
birds of metal met birds of white
escaping from the polar opposite,
blood erupted from my gums
and the sky was crowded.
even after all that didn't happen,
whenever i hear the drone of rain around me
the rain which birthed us all
i see the steps beneath my feet,
a passenger train headed towards the birds
birds as endless as the rain in our dreams
rain from the month of sawdust
