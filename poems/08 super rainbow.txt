starved by dreams of
a metallic rainbow,
this pleasant void we call rome
pulses through my eyes.
(body mind skull)
tie each colour in a knot,
a knot with seven names
through every meadow
shine seven suns yet
the strongest light is
dispelled from within.
as each image disappears,
the past and the future
collapse around me,
to an ever blooming drone
that itself exists within
the droning of the sun
that's expanding as we sing.
black headed moth,
rest your wings and hold my hand
walk upon the rainbow with me.
frame by frame,
i'll drink the sunlight
from your hands until
i feel myself dream.
