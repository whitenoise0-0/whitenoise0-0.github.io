every rhythm you flutter
stretches nausea over skin,
all the leaves you turn over
bleed out the past. 
(virtual and physical)
it's a privilege to 
put a name to the face,
and a desire to
put a face to the name.
a cloud of absence clears the sky
as i vomit out the past,
i hang the future over 
your silvered wings
and enter through the leaves.
black headed moth,
you were there when i left and 
will be there when i leave,
you exhale the mist
with each breath you take,
you choke each flower
that came before you,
for you awaken the moon and
leave the sun to its dreams.
all the leaves you morph into
bring us closer to zero,
are you many ? are you one ?
are you many ? are you one ?
