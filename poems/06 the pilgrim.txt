details of a pilgrimage erupted 
from a mouth which said fuck twice,
ropes in my ribs weigh me down
and wolves feed on my shins.
we played the same chord for four minutes,
mine evoked the void
with a knife through its bicep,
hers evoked the sun
with tears in its eyes.
witnessing her bridges blooming
left my eyes bleeding through my skull.
a cloud of hypnosis covers my heart
and her light still shines in my eyes,
like the collection of dust
the moon has rises on the opposite pole
now that we're far off,
bridges leave me begging for more
but who am i to beg for power ?
for even the beggar has her eyes,
one to look at you and
one that sees it all.
