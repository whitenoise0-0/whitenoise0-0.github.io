as the train crosses a bridge
my attention goes towards what it is crossing over
one of the tributaries of white noise
not even the most skilled can withstand its stream
though some willingly jump into the waters
and let the stream take them wherever it wants
regular people spend their time shredding by the oxbow lake
a few brave souls pull the chain and dive directly into the waters
i push my head against the window
as much as i can to get a look at those who left
how long was i looking for ?
i didn't even notice the trees pass by
