bone marrow of the moon reflects
an endless, nameless face
tangible from the stars,
droning through the past and the future,
splitting the present upon deaf ears
and breaking all thirty one hearts.
it ties our bodies to the grass
with no left or right and
the moth drinks our blood,
as the clouds watch from above 
and the rain washes our heart.
every face never born merged into one 
stretches its scalding middle finger 
towards us across the observable universe.
this false prophet spirals into you
as the worlds collapse and
the stars eat my thoughts,
when god makes peace and i'm left behind.
bound to the grass, an unlucky observer.

butterflies within the sun shine 
a bodyless, soundless light
intangible yet all encompassing,
visible on my eyelids as a colourful mist
during a handshake between love and hate,
clouded behind the dirt of another lover.
it plants seeds and blooms flowers as it flows
through each body mind and skull.
tying the past and the future to your wrist,
seven chariots lead the light
to every bird to ever exist 
and shine through the desert glass 
and flowers of illusion.
no matter which face breathes,
it kills me the same way it was birthed.

the dream,
missing within the trees and 
among what's been undone,
exhales the heat till 
our bodies dissolve.
even after death,
i'll live in your dreams and
stitch your skin back up
from the velvet noise.
see through the glass,
reveal your bliss and
smell each rose that
blooms from my spine,
break each tooth but 
leave one behind,
one for each day of october.
