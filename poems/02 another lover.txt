it had so many flowers 
one for each day of october
they were redder than the seventh circle
of hell and its teeth were 
the best spot for guided meditation
planted 6 years before the sound
of two virgins shredding a drone
we had the longest existing handshake in all of nonexistence
if only our seas mixed
mine of noise, yours of blood
but i didn't think you'd have enough hair for eleven of us
you had so many flowers
none for each night of october
look at them in the chest, all dried up
the pallid mask is all i have to remember you by
we had the shortest nonexisting song in all of existence
was it even real at all ?
i can't help but wonder if i put my faith in the wrong things
i still think of your hair everytime i see red
