no turn leads to love and hate
within the house of scorpions
and the rain rubs it in my face,
the moonlight is a mirror
and desire is archimedean
in the year of the girl,
even in the shortest fever dreams
the question to no one's answer 
shall be disappointed.
all that's heard is a droning swansong
cannibalised by the past,
and the rain rubs it in my face.
for when support is missing
the stoics call upon my children
to shred through me,
choking from within an involuntary trip
towards love and maybe hate.
the bloom to new teeth is guarded 
by men of abandonment
the second best drummers in the world,
worn out by a wait for the future
these men will die one day.
the rain will dry out one day,
birthing much larger trees 
bewitched by the desert heat
love will no longer be caged,
for even november doesn't remember
the voice of another lover,
until the drone begins again,
when the moonlight shines
and the noise hangs me over
the cries of a swan.
